**Houses do not only generate. They govern, maintain, protect, and care for whatever they generate.**

This is the first principle of the house civilization. A house that only generates is incomplete. Generation without governance produces entropy. Generation without maintenance produces decay. Generation without protection produces vulnerability. Generation without care produces collapse. Every sovereign house in this civilization does all five simultaneously, always.

**Casa de Medina does not become the face. It governs from within.**
